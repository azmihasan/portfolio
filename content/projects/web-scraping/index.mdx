Web scraping is the process of automatically extracting data from websites. 
It allows you not just to collect but also automate extracting information like product prices, news articles, or job listings and convert it into a structured format for analysis, comparison and business automation.

### 🧑‍💼 Use Case: Job Market Data Extraction

Developed a web scraper to automatically extract information from job listings — including **job titles**, **required qualifications**, **employer names**, and **locations**.  
This tool helps users or analysts to:

- **Track in-demand skills and roles across industries** 
- **Monitor hiring trends** by employer or region  
- **Build structured datasets** for job market research or career planning

---

### 🛒 Use Case: Product Comparison Across E-commerce Platforms

Built a web scraper to collect **product details, prices, ratings, and availability** from multiple e-commerce websites (e.g., Amazon, eBay, Walmart).  
This solution enables:

- **Real-time price comparison** to find the best deals  
- **Automate data extraction** from page to subpage using **deep scraping**
- **Tracking product availability** across platforms  
- **Analyzing customer ratings and trends** for smarter purchasing decisions  
- Supporting **buyers, researchers, or price-monitoring tools**
