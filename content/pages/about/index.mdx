# About

Azmi Hasan is a motivated Information System Management student at TU Berlin with a strong foundation in IT support, data analysis, and AI engineering. 
Originally from Indonesia, Azmi moved to Germany in 2016 in pursuit of better opportunities and has since built a diverse portfolio through academic and professional experiences.
With hands-on skills in Windows system administration, client support, and process optimization, he has contributed to various companies such as [Indomarkt GmbH](https://indomarkt.com) and [TechBros GmbH](https://techbros.io).
Azmi is passionate about using technology to solve real-world problems and is equally enthusiastic about customer service, sustainability, and cultural exchange.
Through [IDEATEX](https://ideatex.ppijerman.org/), Azmi collaborates with friends and his community to bring together talented individuals and bright minds to discuss and develop solutions for environmental and sustainability challenges. 
Their goal is to contribute meaningfully toward achieving the United Nations’ Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
Fluent in English and proficient in German (C1), he brings a blend of technical know-how and intercultural competence to every team he joins.
